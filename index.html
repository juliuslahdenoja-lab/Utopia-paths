    Research the paths can't rank

    Some research doesn't fit one number. These notes rate it by hand for an Apex fragment farmer, using the effects in the 1.5.7 data.

    What hurts Apex enemies

    Immune: all three

    Spin Blades, Shockwave push, Arc Flail (it can't target them either), Void Nuke pull and Void Nuke drain.

    Knockback

    Blaster is fully immune. Leech and Splitter resist 95%.

    Not immune

    City cannon (ricochets and crits included), Thorns, Air Mines, Smart Missile, Trinity Prism, Stasis Field.

    Bosses

    Spin Blades kill any non-boss enemy on contact; bosses take no blade damage. Bosses weigh 50, tanks 5, everything else 1 for knockback.
